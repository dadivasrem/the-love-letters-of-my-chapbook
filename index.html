Synopsis: The Love Letters of My Chapbook is a poetic chapbook by Rem Jaen D. Krafft's personal experience and creative expression. It incorporates themes of longing, devotion, bittersweetness, and transition from high school life to collegiate ambition. Through poetry, it blends quiet vulnerability with landscape imagery and metaphorical symbols that resemble hardship, incorporating themes of limerence and social struggle.
